About me
======

I am currently a fourth year undergraduate student from [School of Automation Science and Engineering](https://automation.xjtu.edu.cn/), [XJTU](https://xjtu.edu.cn). My research interest includes pattern recognition, artificial intelligence and bioinfomatics. 


Related Undergraduate Resources
======
As an undergraduate student from XJTU, I got relatively good exam marks in various courses. As a student about to graduate, I want to share some of the resources online to help more students master these courses more proficiently. Therefore, I created a new repository on github and share my notes and some of the other materials such as some of the project code. All of the resources are open source and free. The only thing worth noting is that these materials are only suitable for students majoring in automation science or majors related to that.
